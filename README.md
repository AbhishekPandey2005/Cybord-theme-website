# Cortex Research — AX-01

An interactive field guide to AX-01, a fictional augmented human. The centerpiece is a full-body anatomy atlas with 22 selectable regions, from the cranium to the toes. Each entry pairs a description of the human anatomy with a proposed mechanical counterpart.

## Explore the guide

- **Anatomy atlas** — Click a region on the figure, choose it from the menu, or scroll the descriptions. The diagram highlights the corresponding part.
- **Material library** — Examine the ceramic cheek plate, carbon-composite brace, and copper bearing.
- **Motion study** — Move the angle slider to see the neck linkage rotate around its bearing.
- **Prototype archive** — Compare the fictional hand design with a photograph of the e-NABLE Raptor Reloaded prosthesis.

## Run locally

Download the repository and open `index.html` in a browser. The page uses HTML, CSS, SVG, and vanilla JavaScript in one file. The portrait is embedded and the interactive atlas works offline. The prototype photograph loads from Wikimedia Commons and requires an internet connection.

## References

The atlas links to OpenStax’s *Anatomy and Physiology* for the human skeletal anatomy. AX-01’s mechanical systems are fictional. The prototype photograph is credited to Daderot in the archive, alongside the original image and its CC0 dedication.
